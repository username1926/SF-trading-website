Who We Are

SF Trading and Engineering PLC is an Ethiopian engineering company specializing in electrical, mechanical, and automation solutions for industrial, commercial, institutional, and infrastructure projects.

We are committed to providing safe, reliable, innovative, and cost-effective engineering services that meet international standards while creating long-term value for our clients and partners.

Guided by our company philosophy, "Focus on the Solution, Not the Problem," every project is approached with careful planning, technical expertise, and a dedication to delivering practical engineering solutions.
